About me

Turning research ideas into AI that ships

I'm a Data Science student at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT) who likes the unglamorous half of machine learning: clean data, honest evaluation and systems that keep working on real hardware.

Most of my work sits where healthcare meets AI — an offline medical interpreter for clinics, a wake-word assistant for elderly care, bone-age estimation from X-rays and caregiver localisation in nursing homes. Alongside that I compete in international challenges and build product features with startup teams.

I care about leakage-safe validation, reproducibility and fail-safe behaviour: if a model isn't sure, it should say so instead of guessing.

Speech & language AI

ASR, machine translation, TTS and wake-word models — fine-tuned with LoRA and shipped to edge devices.

Medical & health AI

Clinical safety gates, medical NLP, imaging and sensor-based monitoring for care settings.

Edge & on-device ML
